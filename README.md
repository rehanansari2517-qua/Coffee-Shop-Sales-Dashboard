## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Excel to present coffee shop sales data in a visual dashboard. It is designed to help users explore performance, compare products, and identify trends that may support better business decisions.

<details>
<summary><strong>🎯 Project objectives — click to expand</strong></summary>

- Review overall sales performance.
- Explore sales trends over time.
- Compare product and category performance.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Present business information in an accessible dashboard.
- Practise data analysis and dashboard storytelling.

</details>

## 🔎 Business Questions

Use the dashboard and underlying data to investigate questions such as:

| # | Business question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do sales change over time? | Helps identify trends and periods worth investigating. |
| 2 | Which products contribute most to sales? | Supports product performance comparisons. |
| 3 | Which product categories perform well? | Helps compare demand across the product range. |
| 4 | Are there noticeable customer purchasing patterns? | Helps frame questions about customer behaviour. |
| 5 | Which periods or products may need further investigation? | Supports evidence-based follow-up decisions. |

## 📊 Analysis Areas

<details>
<summary><strong>💰 Sales performance</strong></summary>

Explore sales totals and changes across the time periods available in the dataset. Compare periods consistently and investigate any noticeable peaks or declines.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>☕ Product performance</strong></summary>

Compare products using the sales measures available in the workbook. Look for high-performing and lower-performing products, then consider what additional information would help explain their performance.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>👥 Customer purchasing patterns</strong></summary>

Explore customer-related fields, if available, to identify patterns in purchasing behaviour. Avoid drawing conclusions that the dataset cannot support.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>📈 Business interpretation</strong></summary>

Translate observed patterns into follow-up questions and practical recommendations. Validate findings against the underlying data before presenting them as business conclusions.

</details>
